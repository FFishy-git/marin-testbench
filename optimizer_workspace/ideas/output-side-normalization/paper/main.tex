\begin{abstract}
Muon-type optimizers orthogonalize the momentum of each weight matrix and, in
their preconditioned variants, whiten it against the second moment of the
layer's \emph{inputs}. The second moment of the layer's \emph{output cotangents}
is equally available and is not used. Across \nDistinctRuns{} full-budget
training trajectories on a pinned 265M-parameter transformer (2.68B tokens,
eight A100s per run), we study \emph{width-thresholded output normalization}
(\mlpdiag{}), which applies dense spectral output factors at hidden width
($d=512$) and switches to exact elementwise diagonals at MLP-expansion width
($d=2048$), alongside its pure-diagonal limit (\alldiag{}). Four results emerge.
First, output moments help \emph{on top of} input whitening (\mlpdiagsel{} nats
held-out loss across all five training windows and all three seeds, and
$-0.0059$ nats net of a matched execution-path null that absorbs \nullsharedense--\nullsharemlpcfour\%
of raw gains), whereas substituting output for input whitening regresses by
\leftallsel{} nats. Second, checkpoint audits of all 352 EMA moments across 32
layers explain \emph{why} width-thresholding matches or beats full dense roots:
pre-SwiGLU cotangents are Hadamard products whose off-diagonal correlations sit
at the finite-sample floor (median RMS \mechOutGateCorrRmsMed{} and
\mechOutUpCorrRmsMed{}), putting \mechOutGateExpVarLPct\% and
\mechOutUpExpVarLPct\% of $\|L - I\|_F^2$ on the diagonal despite
$\mechOutGateDiagMaxMean\times$ coordinate-scale anisotropy, whereas
residual-stream width-512 cotangents are $97\%$ rotational; instantaneous step
norms match input-side MuonT to \mechMlpdiagGraftNorm{} ($\mechMlpdiagGraftNormPct\%$)
while rotating updates by $\mechMlpdiagGraftDeg^\circ$, ruling out implicit
step-size inflation. Third, eliminating synchronization, backward-wrapper, and
SIMT-FP32 bottlenecks in a one-step-lagged runner (\texttt{fast\_lag}) cuts
calibrated eight-A100 steady-step overhead over vanilla Muon from
$+\sysLegacyMlpVsMuon\%$ to $+\sysFastMlpVsMuonLo\%$--$\sysFastMlpVsMuonHi\%$
($+\sysFastMlpVsMuontLo\%$--$\sysFastMlpVsMuontHi\%$ over MuonT, saving
\sysMemSavedGib\,GiB) for \texttt{mlpdiag\_lag} and
$+\sysFastAllVsMuonLo\%$--$\sysFastAllVsMuonHi\%$ even for dense
\texttt{all\_lag}, crossing the \sysTokenSavingPct\% token-saving wall-clock
break-even threshold. Fourth, short-schedule ($320$-step) learning-rate
screening fails to transfer because its selected rate ($0.064$) regresses
full-budget MuonT itself by \waveTwelveMuontSelDiff{} nats relative to the
recipe rate ($0.0226$), focusing remaining confirmation on a compact full-budget
neighborhood around $0.0226$.
\end{abstract}
