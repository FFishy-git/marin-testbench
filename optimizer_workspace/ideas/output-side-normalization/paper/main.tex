\begin{abstract}
Muon-type optimizers orthogonalize weight momentum and, in preconditioned
variants (\taxid{MuonT}), whiten it against the layer's \emph{input} second
moment while leaving the \emph{output-cotangent} second moment unused. Auditing
all 352 input and cotangent EMA moments across 32 layers and eight checkpoints
($0.13\text{B}$--$2.68\text{B}$ tokens) of baseline \taxid{MuonT} explains why
naive dense two-sided whitening is poorly matched to a transformer: pre-SwiGLU
width-2048 cotangents (\texttt{gate}, \texttt{up}) are zero-mean Hadamard
products whose off-diagonal correlations hug the sampling floor (median RMS
\motOutGateCorrRmsMed{} and \motOutUpCorrRmsMed{}), putting
\motOutGateDiagAnisoMedPct\% and \motOutUpDiagAnisoMedPct\% of centered
anisotropy on the diagonal, whereas residual width-512 cotangents (\texttt{o},
\texttt{down}) are $>99\%$ rotational, post-SwiGLU width-2048 inputs
(\texttt{in:down}) carry $56.6\%$ off-diagonal anisotropy, and cotangent eigenspaces drift
$5\times$--$\motDriftRatioOutVVsInAttn\times$ faster than RMSNormed inputs
($\motFbOutTotal{}$ versus $\motFbInTotal{}$ exact eigendecomposition fallbacks).
Evaluating \nOptimizerArms{} configurations across \nDistinctRuns{} full-budget
trajectories on a pinned 265M-parameter transformer ($2.68\text{B}$ tokens, eight
A100s per run), asymmetric width-thresholding (\taxid{WT-MLP} / \mlpdiag{}, dense
at $d=512$ and inline diagonal on width-2048 outputs) improves held-out loss by
$\mlpdiagsel{}$ nats ($-0.0059$ net of a matched null absorbing
\nullsharedense--\nullsharemlpcfour\% of raw gains) across all five windows and
three seeds at matched step norm (\mechMlpdiagGraftNorm{}, $\mechMlpdiagGraftDeg^\circ$
rotation), while substituting output for input whitening fails ($\leftallsel{}$
nats) and symmetric MLP width-thresholding (\taxid{WT-Sym}, also diagonalizing
\texttt{in:down}) incurs an early constant-LR training penalty despite high
diagonal-root fidelity, then matches final C4 ($\symMlpLagCfourFinalAbs{}$).
This recipe comparison changes inner whitening, outer transport, and shaped
decay together.
On uncontended eight-A100 nodes, our asynchronous one-step-lagged runner
(\texttt{fast\_lag}) cuts full-run steady-step overhead over \taxid{MuonT} from
$+18.0\%$ to $+\sysExecMlpLagVsMuontPct\%$ ($\sysExecMlpLagStepS$\,s,
$\sysExecMlpLagGib$\,GiB) on \taxid{WT-MLP-Lag} and $+\sysExecSymLagVsMuontPct\%$
($\sysExecSymLagStepS$\,s, $\sysExecSymLagGib$\,GiB) on \taxid{WT-Sym-Lag},
enabling \taxid{WT-Sym-Lag} to cross $\text{C4}\le 3.52$ in
$\sysCrossSymLagThreeFiveTwoS$\,s --- ahead of same-wave \taxid{MuonT}
($\sysCrossMuontSymThreeFiveTwoS$\,s), Wave-0 \taxid{MuonT}
($\sysCrossMuontWaveZeroThreeFiveTwoS$\,s), and vanilla \taxid{Muon}
($\sysCrossMuonWaveZeroThreeFiveTwoS$\,s). Finally, 320-step learning-rate
screening over-steps the 5{,}120-step horizon ($\waveTwelveMuontSelDiff{}$ nats on
\taxid{MuonT} at $0.064$ versus $0.0226$), motivating a \confTotalRuns{}-run
\texttt{fast\_lag} confirmation and scaling campaign
(Section~\ref{sec:confirmation}). At full budget, \taxid{Null-WT} closes the
same-runner attribution gap ($\confNullFinalSelDelta{}$ final /
$\confNullLateSelDelta{}$ late-11 selection nats), \taxid{WT-MLP-Lag} beats
\taxid{MuonT} at every matched learning rate ($\confPOneZeroOneSixWtLateSel{}$
and $\confPOneZeroTwoTwoWtLateSel{}$ late-11 selection at $0.016$ and $0.0226$,
winning $11/11$ evaluations in each), replicates across three fresh
post-selection seeds ($\confPTwoWtSelDeltaMean{} \pm \confPTwoWtSelDeltaStd{}$
selection, $\confPTwoWtPalomaDeltaMean{}$ across 16 Paloma domains, $3/3$ seeds
at $+\confPTwoWtStepPct\%$ step time), holds under $4\times$ Chinchilla
overtraining ($80$ TPP, $10.74\text{B}$ tokens: $\confPThreeWtFinalSel{}$ final /
$\confPThreeWtLateSel{}$ late-11 selection, $\confPThreeWtPalomaDelta{}$ Paloma
winning $\confPThreeWtPalomaWins{}/16$ domains at matched $\eta = 0.0106$), and
scales to a $520\text{M}$-non-embedding ($799.6\text{M}$ total) transformer at
$10.74\text{B}$ tokens ($\confPFourWtFinalSel{}$ final / $\confPFourWtLateSel{}$
late-11 selection, $\confPFourWtPalomaDelta{}$ Paloma winning
$\confPFourWtPalomaWins{}/16$ domains at $+\confPFourWtStepPct\%$ step time),
where diagonal-only \taxid{OD-All} regresses slightly
($\confPFourOdFinalSel{}$ selection, $\confPFourOdPalomaDelta{}$ Paloma).
\end{abstract}
