\begin{abstract}
Muon-type optimizers orthogonalize the momentum of each weight matrix and, in
their preconditioned variants, whiten it against the second moment of the
layer's \emph{inputs}. The second moment of the layer's \emph{output cotangents}
is equally available and is not used. Auditing all 352 input and cotangent EMA
moments across 32 layers and eight checkpoints ($0.13\text{B}$--$2.68\text{B}$
tokens) of a pure baseline MuonT trajectory reveals why naive two-sided dense
whitening is mismatched to a transformer: pre-SwiGLU width-2048 cotangents
(\texttt{gate}, \texttt{up}) are Hadamard products whose off-diagonal
correlations sit on the single-update sampling floor (median RMS
\motOutGateCorrRmsMed{} and \motOutUpCorrRmsMed{}), putting
\motOutGateDiagAnisoMedPct\% and \motOutUpDiagAnisoMedPct\% of centered moment
anisotropy on the diagonal (\motOutGateExpVarLMedPct\% and
\motOutUpExpVarLMedPct\% of $\|L-I\|_F^2$, rising to \mechOutGateExpVarLPct\%
and \mechOutUpExpVarLPct\% under active two-sided preconditioning), whereas
residual-stream width-512 cotangents (\texttt{o}, \texttt{down}) are $>99\%$
rotational and cotangent eigenspaces drift $5\times$--$\motDriftRatioOutVVsInAttn\times$
faster than RMSNormed inputs, triggering $\motFbOutTotal{}$ exact
eigendecomposition fallbacks across eight GPUs versus $\motFbInTotal{}$ on
inputs. Guided by this structure, we evaluate \emph{width-thresholded output
normalization} (\taxid{WT-MLP} / \mlpdiag{}, dense at $d=512$ and exact diagonal
at $d=2048$) and its pure-diagonal limit (\taxid{OD-All} / \alldiag{}) across
\nDistinctRuns{} full-budget trajectories on a pinned 265M-parameter transformer
(2.68B tokens, eight A100s per run). Output moments help \emph{on top of} input
whitening ($\mlpdiagsel{}$ nats held-out loss across all five windows and all
three seeds, $-0.0059$ nats net of a matched execution-path null absorbing
\nullsharedense--\nullsharemlpcfour\% of raw gains) and fail when substituted
for it (\taxid{Abl-OutOnly} / \leftall{}, $\leftallsel{}$ nats), while matching
MuonT's instantaneous step norm to \mechMlpdiagGraftNorm{}
($\mechMlpdiagGraftNormPct\%$) at a $\mechMlpdiagGraftDeg^\circ$ directional
rotation. Eliminating synchronization, backward-wrapper, and SIMT-FP32
bottlenecks in a one-step-lagged runner (\texttt{fast\_lag}) cuts calibrated
eight-A100 step overhead over vanilla Muon from $+\sysLegacyMlpVsMuon\%$ to
$+\sysFastMlpVsMuonLo\%$--$\sysFastMlpVsMuonHi\%$
($+\sysFastMlpVsMuontLo\%$--$\sysFastMlpVsMuontHi\%$ over MuonT, saving
\sysMemSavedGib\,GiB) for \taxid{WT-MLP-Lag} (\mlpdiaglag{}) and
$+\sysFastAllVsMuonLo\%$--$\sysFastAllVsMuonHi\%$ for dense \taxid{DT-All-Lag}
(\denselag{}), crossing the \sysTokenSavingPct\% token-saving break-even line.
Finally, 320-step learning-rate screening fails to transfer because its selected
rate ($0.064$) regresses full-budget MuonT itself by \waveTwelveMuontSelDiff{}
nats relative to the recipe rate ($0.0226$), focusing remaining confirmation on
a compact full-budget sweep around $0.0226$.
\end{abstract}
