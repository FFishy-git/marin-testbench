## 1. Settled Research Scope & Candidate Selection

Rather than treating 15 exploratory arms on equal footing, the paper's scope is now anchored on **Width-Thresholded Output-Cotangent Normalization**:

1. **Primary Method — `inner_outer_mlpdiag` (and 1-step lagged `inner_outer_mlpdiag_lag`):**
   - Retains full dense MuonT whitening ($R, \hat R$) on all inputs, applies **dense spectral output roots ($L, \hat L$)** at hidden width ($d = 512$: `q, k, v, o, down`), and switches to **exact elementwise diagonal roots ($L^{\text{diag}}, \hat L^{\text{diag}}$)** at MLP-expansion width ($d = 2048$: `gate, up`).
   - Achieves **`-0.0066` nats** held-out selection loss (`-0.0059` net of matched execution-path null) and **`-0.0061` nats** late C4 (`-0.0040` net of null), winning across all 5 training windows, all 3 seeds (`-0.0061`, `-0.0042`, `-0.0040` late C4), and 1M batch size (`-0.0035` late C4).
2. **Pure-Diagonal Speed Variant — `inner_outer_leftdiag`:**
   - Applies exact diagonal output roots ($L^{\text{diag}}, \hat L^{\text{diag}}$) across all 7 projections (`-0.0045` selection, ~68% of `mlpdiag`'s gain) with zero output eigendecompositions and non-decaying 5-window gains.
3. **Demoted Trap Arm — `outer_leftdiag` / `outer_leftdiag_fused`:**
   - Dropping the pre-Newton–Schulz inner factor $L$ leaves only **`-0.00044` nats** net of its Wave-10 matched null (`-0.00245` vs. `-0.00201` selection) while saving `<1%` step time. Pre-orthogonalization scaling $L$ ($-1/2$ power inside $\mathcal{N}_{10}$) carries the real signal.

## 2. Offline Checkpoint Mechanism Audit (`sections/mechanism.tex`)

Auditing all **32 layers (8 ranks, 352 FP32 EMA matrices)** of `wave1/inner_outer_all` at `step_002500` (cross-checked at `step_000500` and `step_005120`) resolved both major theoretical questions in the previous draft at **zero GPU training cost** (generated by `scripts/analyze_mechanism.py`):

### A. Why `inner_outer_mlpdiag` Matches/Beats `inner_outer_all` (`gate`/`up` Hadamard Decorrelation)
- **Off-diagonal correlations sit at the finite-sample floor:** Across all 32 layers, width-2048 pre-SwiGLU cotangents $B_{\text{gate}}$ and $B_{\text{up}}$ concentrate **`81.9%` and `75.5%`** of $\|B\|_F^2$ on their diagonal (medians **`93.2%` and `87.3%`**). Their median off-diagonal correlation RMS is **`0.0050` and `0.0092`** (`0.0062` and `0.0104` across bulk Layers 4–31), matching the single-step finite-sample floor $1/\sqrt{36{,}608} = 0.0052$.
- **Near-exact diagonal preconditioner fidelity:** $L^{\text{diag}}$ aligns with dense $L$ at cosine **`0.9896` (`gate`, median `0.9971`)** and **`0.9826` (`up`, median `0.9924`)**, capturing **`93.2%` and `85.4%`** of $\|L - I\|_F^2$ (medians **`95.1%` and `90.1%`**).
- **Largest coordinate-scale anisotropy in the network:** At the same time, `gate` and `up` have diagonal CV **`7.06` and `5.07`** (`max/mean = 278x` and `173x`), explaining why omitting them (`inner_outer_hidden`, `||L - I||_F / ||L||_F = 0.432–0.452`) loses half the benefit.
- **Schur/Hadamard mechanism vs. `in_down` ($d=2048$):** Pre-SwiGLU cotangents $\delta_{\text{up}} = (\delta_{\text{down}} W_{\text{down}}) \odot \operatorname{SiLU}(g)$ and $\delta_{\text{gate}} = (\delta_{\text{down}} W_{\text{down}}) \odot (u \odot \operatorname{SiLU}'(g))$ are elementwise products of a zero-mean residual cotangent with a forward gate; under Schur multiplication ($\Sigma_{ij} \approx \Sigma^{\text{bwd}}_{ij}\Sigma^{\text{fwd}}_{ij}$), weak off-diagonal correlations multiply toward zero while diagonal variances multiply together. By contrast, post-SwiGLU `in_down` at the *same* width 2048 has non-zero positive $\operatorname{SiLU}$ means, giving `54x` higher median correlation energy (`2.72` vs. `0.050`).
- **Residual-stream outputs (`o`, `down`) and RMSNormed inputs (`attn`, `o`, `mlp`) are rotational:** `out_o` and `out_down` have nearly uniform channel variance ($\mathrm{CV}(D) = 0.18, 0.17$), so $L^{\text{diag}}$ explains only **`3.0%` and `2.9%`** of $\|L - I\|_F^2$ (missing `97%` of their rotational conditioning—explaining why `mlpdiag` beats `leftdiag`), while width-512 inputs have `92%–95%` off-diagonal energy ($R^{\text{diag}}$ explains `8.8%–28.6%`, explaining why `inner_outer_all_diag` regresses by `+0.0180` nats).

### B. Ruling Out Implicit Step-Size Inflation (Norm-Grafting Check)
- Across all 224 matrices at `step_002500`, the instantaneous direction-norm ratio $\|D_{\text{cand}}(U)\|_F / \|D_{\text{in}}(U)\|_F$ on identical $(U, R, \hat R)$ states is **`0.9854 ± 0.0106` (`-1.46%`)** for `mlpdiag`, **`0.9943` (`-0.57%`)** for `leftdiag`, and **`0.9833` (`-1.67%`)** for `inner_outer_all` (`0.9809` at `step_005120`).
- At that nearly identical (slightly *smaller*) norm, `mlpdiag` rotates the update direction by **`22.4°`** (`cos = 0.9218`, spanning `17.0°–26.1°`), and equilibrium block weight norm $\|W\|_F$ is **`-3.00%`** smaller than `muont` (`null_mlpdiag`: `-0.11%`). Output normalization is pure directional reorientation, not a hidden learning-rate increase.

### Three-Pillar Redesign
1. **True Async Lag + Opportunistic Mid-Step Fallback Resolution (`-82 ms` on `mlpdiag_lag`, `-209 ms` on `all_lag`):**
   - Replaced `train.py`'s per-step global `torch.cuda.synchronize()` with main-stream-only synchronization (`current_stream().synchronize()`), protected `output_rows` via a zero-extra-memory CUDA event right after `syrk` (`~1.4 ms`), and resolved flagged root repairs + exact `cuSOLVER eigh` fallbacks + FP16 factor splits opportunistically during microbatch $k \ge 1$ on a priority-0 side stream ~`700 ms` before `step()` consumes them.
2. **Grouped Block-Level Triton Capture + Deterministic 2D-Tiled Inline Diagonal (`8.4x` faster capture, `-81.8 ms`, `-1.11 GiB` VRAM):**
   - Replaced 224 `RowObservedLinear` wrappers (`896` custom-op AOTAutograd partitions and `2,688` ATen launches/step) with 4 view-transparent hooks per block, accumulating `gate`/`up` diagonal squares directly in registers (`BLOCK_R=16, BLOCK_C=64`, zero atomics) without allocating the 64 `[4576, 2048]` BF16 buffers.
3. **Compiled Ozaki FP16-Split Tensor-Core Update Stack (`-13.3 ms` to `-17.5 ms`):**
   - Replaced SIMT FP32 `torch.bmm` (`19.5 TFLOPS` under `allow_tf32=False`) with `@torch.compile(options=PR._EXACT)` FP16-split Tensor-Core products (`312 TFLOPS`), fused post-$\mathcal{N}_{10}$ right GEMMs from 3 to 2, and removed per-step `.tolist()` host finite syncs.
4. **Hardware Verification & Wall-Clock Implication:**
   - Passed `42/42` CPU tests, `11/11` two-rank NCCL `experiment.gpu_checks` gates, `1.02e-7` relative update parity vs. `FastTonerCore`, and **bitwise-exact (`0.00e+00`) 24-step uninterrupted vs. step-22 resumed checkpoint continuation** across all 96 (`mlpdiag_lag`) and 99 (`all_lag`) rank tensors.
   - Because `inner_outer_mlpdiag_lag` crosses `C4 <= 3.52` in **`5.26%` fewer tokens** than `MuonT`, cutting step overhead over `MuonT` to `+1.6%–2.1%` crosses the wall-clock break-even threshold (`0.9474 × 1.018 ≈ 0.965`, ~**3.5% projected wall-clock speedup**).
