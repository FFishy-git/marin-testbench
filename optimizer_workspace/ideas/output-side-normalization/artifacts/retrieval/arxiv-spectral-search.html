Second, shrinking the largest singular values is what keeps the parameter spectrum flat; this is cheap, and it is not what drives the loss. Third, AdamW differs from Muon mainly in how slowly it builds structure, which explains its slower start and why warmup helps AdamW but only hurts Muon. △ Less
